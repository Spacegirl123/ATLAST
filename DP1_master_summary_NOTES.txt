================================================================================
 DP1_master_summary.csv  --  column index
 (one row per asteroid; produced by LSST_DP1_V5.ipynb)
================================================================================
Notes:
  * "reduced magnitude" / "H" = distance- + phase-angle-corrected absolute mag.
  * P1/P2/P3 and cand1/2/3 are the method's top-3 periods, ranked best-first.
  * "#" below means the rank (1, 2, 3) -- each row of columns repeats 3x.
  * Blank cells mean "not available / not applicable" for that object/period.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
IDENTIFICATION & SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
number             Numbered designation (blank if the object is only provisional).
name               The object's designation (always present; used as its key).
filters            Which filters have data, e.g. "g;i;r".
n_points           Number of observations after outlier clipping.
n_nights           Number of distinct observing nights.
baseline_days      Time span of the observations (days).
H_abs_mean         Mean reduced absolute magnitude H over all points.
H_abs_std          Std-dev of H -- rough total scatter (rotation+noise) before fitting.
obs_per_filter     Per-filter observation counts, e.g. "g:96;i:57;r:143".
                   (Colours now live in DP1_combined_agreement.csv, from the MBLS
                   band offsets -- the old median_* columns were removed.)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MULTIBAND LOMB-SCARGLE  (LS)  --  top 3 periods, # = 1,2,3
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ls2_P#_raw         Raw LS periodogram peak period (h), before peak-doubling.
ls2_P#_npeaks      Maxima per cycle in the folded model (used for peak-doubling).
ls2_P#             Reported LS rotation period (h) = peak-doubling-corrected.
ls2_P#_power_raw   LS power at the raw peak (0-1); detection strength.
ls2_P#_amp         Light-curve amplitude at the corrected period (mag).
ls2_P#_unc         Approx period uncertainty at the corrected period (h).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PHASE DISPERSION MINIMIZATION  (PDM)  --  top 3 periods, # = 1,2,3
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
pdm_P#             PDM period (h), used as-found (no peak-doubling).
pdm_P#_theta       PDM theta at that period (coverage-penalised; LOWER = better).
pdm_P#_amp         Fold amplitude at that period (mag).
pdm_P#_unc         Approx period uncertainty (h) (== raw; PDM has no peak-doubling).
pdm_P#_coverage_pct  % of the 20 phase bins with >=2 points at that period.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
FOURIER SEARCH  --  top 3 candidates, # = 1,2,3
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
fourier_cand#_P        Reported Fourier period (h) = peak-corrected.
fourier_cand#_P_raw    Raw Fourier period (h), before peak correction.
fourier_cand#_k        Fourier order (number of harmonics) used for this candidate.
fourier_cand#_sigma    Weighted dispersion sigma of the fit (LOWER = better).
fourier_cand#_npeaks   Maxima per cycle in the model (used for peak-doubling).
fourier_cand#_amp      Fold amplitude at the corrected period (mag).
fourier_cand#_redchi2  Reduced chi-square of the Fourier fit.
fourier_cand#_P_unc    Approx period uncertainty at the corrected period (h).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SOLAR PHASE CURVE  (IAU H,G fit; rotation subtracted first)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
phase_H            Absolute magnitude H from the H,G phase-curve fit.
phase_G            Slope parameter G from the fit (0-1).
phase_n_nights     Number of nightly-median points used in the fit.
phase_rms          RMS residual of the H,G fit (mag).
phase_rot_P        Rotation period subtracted before the phase-curve fit (h).
phase_alpha_min    Minimum solar phase angle observed (deg).
phase_alpha_max    Maximum solar phase angle observed (deg).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MISC
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
comment            Free-text flags (e.g. day-night period aliases).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
JPL SMALL-BODY DATABASE (SBDB) ENRICHMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
orbit_class        SBDB orbit-class code: MBA, IMB, OMB, MCA, APO, AMO, ATE, TNO...
                   ("not_found" / "error" if the SBDB lookup failed).
neo                Near-Earth object?  Y / N.
pha                Potentially Hazardous Asteroid?  Y / N.
rotation_period_h  Known rotation period from SBDB/LCDB (h); blank if none published
                   -- your cross-check against a previously measured period.
